Institution-sourced steps only. Society guidance lives under Guidelines & References.
Key Principles
No institutional protocol document is on file for subarachnoid hemorrhage. See Guidelines & References for the current society guidance.
1
Immediate Actions (First 6 Hours)
1
BP control
The institutional acute stroke algorithm directs BP control for SAH but does not specify a target, agent, or dose. See Guidelines & References for the current society guidance.
2
Reverse coagulopathy
Screen for anticoagulant/antiplatelet exposure and reverse before the aneurysm is secured — use the coagulopathy-reversal pathway
3
Neurosurgery consult
Consult neurosurgery and admit to neurocritical care, per the institutional acute stroke algorithm.
4
Document Hunt-Hess grade
Record the admission Hunt-Hess grade with the consult (Hunt & Hess / WFNS calculators are under Calculators)
