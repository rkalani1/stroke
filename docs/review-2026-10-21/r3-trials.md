### 8.2 Choice of the STEP bound (30 h, an app limit)

- **The registry sets no deadline.** It bounds STEP's EVT domain by presentation (≤ 24 h from LKW) and by imaging-to-puncture time (≤ 2 h, with imaging repeated when more than 120 minutes pass before randomization). It states no randomization deadline, so any upper bound after 24 h is a screening convention. I labelled it as one in the error text, the pending line, the window text and `sourceGaps`.
- **Why 30 h.** A patient who presents at the latest permitted time has qualifying imaging and a 2-h puncture window. If randomization slips past 120 minutes, the registry repeats imaging, which allows one more 2-h window. That is about 28 h; 30 h leaves time for imaging and consent.
- **What 30 h does.** A patient within 30 h who presented within 24 h stays a potential match, and the puncture and chronic-occlusion items stay to confirm. Beyond 30 h, STEP is never a candidate.
- **Why not the reviewers' 48 h.** On day 2, the "Presented ≤ 24 h" fact is Yes for most inpatients, and day 2 is when study facts are filled in for VERIFY (consent window 24–96 h). A 48-h bound would still list STEP as a potential match for patients no one is evaluating for EVT. The lead asked for the conservative direction, and 30 h confines that exposure to the 6 h after the latest permitted presentation.
- **The cost.** A patient who presented within 24 h and is still being considered for STEP after 30 h shows "not met". The reason names the app limit, so the team can check the protocol.
- **Presets.** The "24h – 7d" preset may still fall within 30 h, so with presentation recorded it stays insufficient information rather than not met. The "7 – 30d", "30 – 180d" and "> 6mo" presets are not met.
