## 3. What changed and why

Two commits. Every new wording rests on a passage read today (quoted in section 4).

1. **thrombolytic-choice caution** (expanded-vascular.json).
   - Old: "A positive trial does not establish local approval or availability of a newer thrombolytic."
   - New: "US labels cover alteplase and (since February 2025) tenecteplase within 3 h of onset; the tenecteplase label uses five weight bands (15–25 mg), above 0.25 mg/kg below 100 kg (0.33 mg/kg at 45 kg). A positive trial does not establish local approval or availability of a newer thrombolytic."
   - Source added: Grotta & Haley, *Stroke Vasc Interv Neurol* 2025, PMID 41573708 (type Report, checkedAt 2026-10-21, access names PMC12697625 and says the FDA label was not reachable).
   - consider[0] was trimmed to keep bytes small: "Use the agent and dose source your protocol specifies; dose by weight with the calculator."
   - The "above 0.25 mg/kg below 100 kg" statement is arithmetic on the label table: each band equals 0.25 mg/kg only at its upper bound. The new test checks it at every 0.1 kg from 30 to 99.9 kg. The calculator's `fda-label` mode reproduces the table; calculators.js was not edited.
2. **malignant-edema consider[2]**: "Avoid hypothermia, barbiturates and corticosteroids for swelling (COR 3: Harm); IV glibenclamide did not help at ages 18–70 (COR 3: No Benefit)." Basis: archive ais-2026-187 and ais-2026-186, and the CHARM abstract.
3. **acute-bp**:
   - consider[1] now reads "For neurological deterioration without IVT or EVT, ESO 2025 discourages routine vasopressors to raise BP (low certainty)", from the ESO abstract.
   - The summary now reads "Cochrane 2026 (after IVT or EVT; targets <160) found probably higher mortality (RR 1.19, 95% CI 1.08–1.32)".
   - consider[0] now reads "After alteplase for mild-to-moderate stroke, SBP 130–140 did not improve 90-day function…", from ais-2026-75 and the ENCHANTED abstract.
   - checkedAt moved to 2026-10-21 for the three sources re-read today (42095756, 30739745, 42445947). Their access strings were unchanged and remain accurate.
4. **ich-management caution**: "ESO 2026 consensus" became "ESO 2025 consensus" (same length).

Generated data/clinical-reference.json grows by **+619 bytes** (511,632 → 512,251; built locally and reverted).
