## 3. Changes and reasons (clinical wording)

- **EVT mRS 2–4.** The recommendation populations come straight from the archived rows. -118: "ICA or M1 … within 6 hours, NIHSS 6 or greater, ASPECTS 6 or greater, and prestroke mRS of 2 … reasonable" (IIa, B-NR). -119: the same with "prestroke mRS of 3-4 … might be reasonable" (IIb, B-NR). -114 and -117 require prestroke mRS 0–1. No archived row covers mRS 2–4 with NIHSS <6 or ASPECTS <6, or mRS 5 at all. The screen now cites a COR only for the -118/-119 population. Outside it, the message names the criteria and gives no class.
- **TIA MRI.** -12: "Multimodal brain MRI is the preferred method … ideally should be obtained within 24 hours of symptom onset." -15: when MRI cannot be obtained acutely, "hospital admission for MRI … transferring … or arranging a timely (ideally <24 hours) outpatient MRI". No statement calls MRI not indicated, so the gap stays open with a reason prompt. -13 ("DWI positivity is associated with a >6-fold increased risk") is why an unestablished DWI "No" must not count as reviewed.
- **Large-core hemorrhage clause.** Removed rather than reworded. The message reports guideline coverage, row -114 is silent on hemorrhage, and the pooled symptomatic-ICH evidence conflicts (§4).

## 6. Protocols observations (report only)

1. **The projection rounds the CTP values before inclusive Protocols thresholds.** `protocolEncounter` (`src/workspace-state.js:114`) passes `ctpRatio: Math.round(total / core * 100) / 100` and `ctpMismatchVolMl: Math.round((total - core) * 10) / 10`. `src/institutional-protocols.js:387-388` then tests `ratio >= 1.2` and `mismatchVol >= 10`. Reproduced with core 49.9 mL and total 59.86 mL: the true ratio is 1.1996 and the true mismatch 9.96 mL, yet they project as 1.2 and 10, which meet both inclusive gates. The EXTEND registry (NCT01580839, re-read 2026-10-22) uses strict "greater than 1.2" and "greater than 10ml". The local Protocols thresholds are inclusive by design (see the comment in `encounter-clinical-review.js`), so the open question is only the rounding before comparison. Both lines are hashed and locked, so the change belongs to the owner.
2. **The Protocols EVT cards offer pre-stroke mRS 6 (death).** `src/pocket-cards.jsx` lines 290 (anterior), 333 (M2) and 360 (basilar) list `['0' … '6']`, and the anterior card writes the value back to the Encounter through `app.jsx` `onChange` (`preMRS` → `premorbidMRS`). The Protocols IVT card (line 164) lists 0–5 and shows 6 only when it arrives from the Encounter. A pre-stroke mRS of 6 is not a state of a living patient (van Swieten 1988, PMID 3363593: the scale "has six grades (0-5)"; grade 6 is the later outcome convention). The Encounter now keeps such a value visible rather than hiding it (`104a665`); the cards themselves are frozen and unchanged.

Only the readiness panel treats it as unreviewed. A note-line flag would cost roughly 100 B more of initial JS, and the unit is at +224/+240 of a +250 cap, so it was not added; the lead may add a `flag` on the `tiaDwiPositive` field in `encounter-details.js` if budget allows.
- **Invalid EVT times:** the EVT decision reconcile flag goes on the puncture line when a puncture time is entered, else on the reperfusion line when a reperfusion time is entered, else on the "EVT puncture: not documented" line. When the chosen line holds an unusable time (invalid, future or ambiguous), that line keeps only its reason (pinned by `tests/encounter-2026-10-21.test.js`), so the decision flag is not shown until the time is corrected. The unusable time itself is already flagged "correct before interpretation".
