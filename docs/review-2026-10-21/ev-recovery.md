## 3. What changed and why

All edits are in `src/reference/*.json` (commit `ed11006`), applied by script with exact old-value assertions. Every changed clinical statement traces to a passage read on 2026-10-21 (section 4).

- **recovery**: summary now reads "AHA/ASA AIS 2026: formal inpatient interdisciplinary assessment and rehabilitation matched to the patient (COR 1); no high-dose, very early mobilization within 24 h (COR 3: Harm); screen for depression … (COR 1). SSRIs are not recommended for motor or functional recovery (COR 3: No Benefit): fluoxetine 20 mg daily for 6 months did not improve mRS and increased fractures (FOCUS, AFFINITY, EFFECTS)." Three trial sources added. The caution says grades are from AIS 2026 and the August 2026 AHA/ASA rehabilitation guideline was not reviewed. The coverage entry `aha-stroke-rehabilitation-2026` now describes today's abstract read.
- **psychosocial-recovery**: "AHA 2026" → "AHA/ASA AIS 2026" for ais-2026-174/-175.
- **pediatric-stroke**: EVT at age ≥6 within 6 h, or 6–24 h with salvageable tissue (2a); 28 days–6 years within 24 h with salvageable tissue (2b).
- **preexisting-disability**: "ICA or M1 occlusion within 6 h with NIHSS ≥6 and ASPECTS ≥6".
- **driving-work**: AAN 2025 positions 4, 5 and 7 (3 seizure-free months ordinarily, extended individually; provoked seizures unlikely to recur may need no interval; pause driving during and after an antiseizure-medication taper unless another is started).
- **maternal-stroke**: AHA 2026 abstract wording ("should not delay evidence-based … treatment"; "CT, CTA and noncontrast MRI are safe"); "no reperfusion RCTs in pregnancy: ESO 2022 expert consensus supports thrombectomy and, after individual risk–benefit review, IVT". Coverage `eso-stroke-in-women-2022` records the PMC full-text read.
- **antiamyloid-vascular-risk**: ARIA edema can mimic stroke and may be missed on CT/CTA; fatal post-IVT hemorrhages reported; AURs advise against thrombolysis (donanemab: unless MRI unequivocally excludes ARIA); donanemab AUR: consider thrombectomy without thrombolysis for LVO. Caution: consensus, not trial evidence; lecanemab AUR stops infusions if anticoagulation becomes necessary; standard-dose antiplatelets allowed. Sources added: lecanemab AUR (PMC10313141), donanemab AUR (PMC12180672), AHA ARIA Summit proceedings (abstract only).
- **vte-prevention**, **stroke-pneumonia**, **inpatient-care**, **imaging-follow-up**, **poststroke-dysphagia**: population, grade-category and label corrections listed in section 2.
- **spasticity**, **aphasia**, **visual-recovery**, **motor-recovery**: graded recommendations added from the sources the cards already cite; source access notes now state the PMC full text or abstract read.
- **vns-rehab** (study): limits add the 2026 Cochrane review (10 RCTs, 7 transcutaneous; mostly high risk of bias; very uncertain) and the uncontrolled 2-year post hoc follow-up; two sources added.
- **goals-of-care**: reciprocal related link to Neuroprognostication, which carries the ICH DNAR and prognosis-timing caveats (ich-2022-94/-95, NCS 2023).
- **checkedAt**: changed to 2026-10-21 only on sources re-read today; access text says what was read.
- **Byte budget**: eleven review questions (spasticity, aphasia, poststroke-primary-care, rehab-access, cancer-stroke, perioperative-stroke, sex-gender-stroke, lifelong-brain-health, stroke-pneumonia, sickle-cell-stroke, psychosocial-recovery) and one closing sentence (inpatient-medical-complications) that restated their own card were removed to pay for additions; each card keeps ≥2 questions.
- **Tests updated (assertion change explained)**: the review cutoff in `tests/clinical-reference-data.test.js`, `tests/evidence-ivt-bp-antiplatelet-content.test.js` and `tests/ich-sah-evidence-review-2026-10-17.test.js` moved from 2026-10-17 to 2026-10-21, because sources re-read today carry `checkedAt: 2026-10-21` and the validator rejects dates after `now`. No other assertion changed. Other agents re-dating sources today need the same one-line change.

Byte delta: `data/clinical-reference.json` (generated, then reverted before commit) 511,632 → 513,847 bytes (+2,215 raw). Service-worker precache 2,337,700 / 2,411,725 bytes. No JavaScript or CSS changed, so the JS and tailwind budgets are unchanged.
