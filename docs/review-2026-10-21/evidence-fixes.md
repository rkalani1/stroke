# evidence-fixes — 2026-10-22

Unit: evidence-fixes. It acts on the independent verifiers' findings for the merged Evidence changes: `findings-evidence.json` (r1_flagged: 67 items from units ev-recovery and ev-acute; bottom_lines_flagged: 48 items, one per flagged Evidence topic). Branch `worktree-agent-ae421cc530fb110d5`. The worktree started at 7.8.0 and was fast-forwarded (`git merge --ff-only`) to the integrated 7.9.0 release candidate `8af8c59` before any edit. The content and test changes are in commit `8541ec1`; this report is committed after it.

Method: I did not apply any fix blindly. For every item I re-read the cited primary source on 2026-10-22, then applied, modified or rejected the proposed fix. Any claim new to the card was added only after I had read its supporting passage that day. Access is as in the earlier units. Publisher sites, PMC web pages, DailyMed/FDA and the AHA/ESC pages were blocked. Sources were read through the PubMed connector (abstracts and metadata, and PMC full text where `get_full_text_article` returned a body). Where the app relies on the earlier full-text transcription, I read it at `git show 4f8e99d:data/guidelines/<file>.json`. That is archive scope: secondary, and used only where the guideline full text is not reachable. The ledger labels it "archived transcription".

Bottom lines: `bottomLine()` in src/reference-search.js shows the first sentence of `summary` alone in the collapsed Evidence row. Every changed bottom line is still one sentence (tested for all 92 topics). Where a qualifier moved into the first sentence, the later duplicate was trimmed.
