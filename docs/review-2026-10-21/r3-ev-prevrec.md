I also added a test assertion that the Encounter's absolute tier is labelled "Generally contraindicated".

**Method.** For each finding I re-checked the text at HEAD and re-read the primary source rather than relying on the reviewer's quotation. Sources were PubMed abstracts, PMC full text, ClinicalTrials.gov records, and the archived guideline transcriptions at `git show 4f8e99d:data/guidelines/*.json`. The archive is secondary to the full texts, which are not reachable. I treated each proposed fix as a suggestion: COR/LOE, population and qualifiers stay exact, and where two wordings are equally accurate I used the shorter. A `checkedAt` changed to 2026-10-22 only for a source re-read that day, and its `access` note says what was read.

## 6. Unresolved uncertainties

1. **CHAMPION-AF ischemic stroke.** The NEJM full text is unreachable. Two reviews report an ischemic-stroke excess with closure:
   - PMID 42747337: "numerically more frequent with LAAC (HR 1.61)";
   - PMID 42739532 (PMC full text): "numerically more often".

   Three 2026 meta-analyses disagree on whether the pooled excess is significant:
   - Stroke, PMID 42817893: OR 1.41 (1.07–1.86);
   - J Cardiovasc Electrophysiol, PMID 42693652: RR 1.35, P=0.08;
   - JACC EP, PMID 42501028: "not statistically significant".

   Secondary summaries cannot be the sole basis for a clinical change, so the card instead states the population limit and that the 5-year ischemic-stroke endpoint is pending. **Lead decision:** add a labelled secondary-report clause once the primary report can be read.

The ESC/EACTS task-force review abstract (PMID 40608489) says surgery "might be delayed up to 4 weeks". The publisher text is not reachable to settle the wording, so the card follows the transcription.

   The archive records ESC corrections 37738322, 38086544 and 39824219 as applied, with none affecting Table 17. That is archive scope only, so the card's "not reconciled" source note is left as it was.
