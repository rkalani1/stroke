# Complete cardiometabolic guideline recommendation coverage — 2026-09-06

This review replaces the prior four dyslipidemia summaries and 19 selected hypertension comparison-table extracts with every formal numbered recommendation in the two full guidelines. It covers all populations and topics in the source recommendation tables, not a stroke-selected subset. Protocols and shared treatment algorithms were not edited.

## Source and accounting method

- Dyslipidemia: 131 unique formal recommendations in 38 tables. The full Circulation PDF contains 48 COR/LOE table fragments including continued tables. Every numbered COR/LOE row was read and matched to one library entry; the independent source inventory also found 131 graded rows.
- Hypertension: 108 unique formal recommendations in 30 tables. The complete Circulation co-publication contains 38 COR/LOE table fragments. PDF table-cell extraction and an independent complete-text grade scan both yielded 108 rows.
- All C-EO recommendations remain formal recommendations and are counted once. Neither document has a separately designated ungraded good-practice or consensus-statement set: sourceConsensusCount is 0. Mentions of other expert-consensus publications are citations, not additional recommendations in these documents.
- What Is New comparison columns, take-home messages, repeated continuation headings, diagrams, drug-reference tables, explanatory narrative, and reference lists are not counted as additional formal rows. Narrative-only sections such as ancestry, chronic inflammatory disease, polygenic scores, and hypertension comorbidities that defer to other guidelines remain available in the complete source; no fabricated recommendation grades were assigned to them.
- Each library row records sourceSection, sourceRecommendationNumber, pdfPage, original COR/LOE, and an original factual summary. Each sourceSections inventory item records the source table title, row count, local numbers, and page range. The fixture records all 239 source grade/page signatures independently of the prose summaries.
- Physical PDF page numbers refer to the complete ahead-of-print editions identified below, not final journal e-page numbering. The canonical guideline DOI/PMID remain the official published source; hypertension uses the matching complete Circulation co-publication for physical-page accounting.
- Source tables were reviewed through extracted text/cells; rendered checks were used for continued tables and risk/grade alignment. Automated metadata/grade reconciliation does not itself establish the clinical accuracy of a summary. All 239 summaries were compared with their source rows for population, direction, strength, thresholds and principal exceptions. This is an inventory of guideline recommendations, not a new evidence review or an update to treatment algorithms.

## Corrections and currency limitations

- **Dyslipidemia:** the full official correction [CIR1457](https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/pdf/10.1161/CIR.0000000000001457?download=true), PMID42330109, was retrieved through the indexed publisher source. It changes the take-home ApoB TG threshold to ≥150 mg/dL, hypertriglyceridemia rows5/6 figure references to16/17, and reviewer-name spelling. COR/LOE and formal treatment wording are unchanged. The erroneous take-home threshold is absent; the two corrected figure references and correction source are recorded on their rows.
- **Hypertension HYP0257 / PMID41259465:** prior verified notice retained. Comparison/grading-key presentation, aprocitentan supporting prose and figure annotations do not add or change the formal recommendation inventory.
- **Hypertension HYP0264 / PMID42160500:** prior verified labetalol Table26 interval correction retained; the drug-dose reference table is outside the formal recommendation inventory. The accessible co-publication correction [CIR1448](https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/pdf/10.1161/CIR.0000000000001448) confirms the 10-minute IV injection interval change. No unverified equivalence between notices is assumed.
- **Hypertension HYP0262 / PMID41984986:** the complete [primary correction notice](https://www.ahajournals.org/doi/pdf/10.1161/HYP.0000000000000262) became available through the publisher's indexed text during integration. Its only change swaps the misplaced Table 16 column entries: thiazide-type diuretics/RAS blockers are the antihypertensives, and lithium is the interacting drug. This reference table is not reproduced in the 108 formal recommendations, so no row requires alteration. The notice is retained with resolved status and explicit scope. HYP0264 was also checked against the complete primary notice: the corrected labetalol injection interval is 10 minutes, not 2 minutes.
