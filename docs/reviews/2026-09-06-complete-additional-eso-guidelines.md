# Additional ESO guideline completeness reconciliation — 2026-09-06

## Scope and method

S1: This follow-on review reconciles all formal recommendation/no-recommendation statements and all formal expert-consensus statements in six additional ESO guidelines. Protocols, the application shell, generated data and bundles are outside this edit. S2: Full primary article bodies and synoptic tables were compared with existing rows. The IVT body was checked separately because its synopsis omits formal uncertainty boxes. Existing source text was retained where correct; new material uses concise factual summaries. S3: Source counts distinguish evidence-based/formal statements from expert opinion. Narrative research discussion and ungraded supporting paragraphs are not promoted to formal recommendations. S5: Ten focused regression tests pass; global integration is owned by the parent review.

## Concrete corrections

- IVT: added seven formal very-low-certainty uncertainty statements: minor non-disabling LVO, rapidly improving but still disabling symptoms, recent NOAC with low measured activity, dabigatran reversal before alteplase, prior intracranial haemorrhage, ischaemic stroke in the preceding three months, and intracerebral dissection. The eighth formal uncertainty (recent STEMI) already existed. The 4.5–9-hour direct-to-thrombectomy-centre failure to reach consensus is a consensus outcome, not a graded formal recommendation. This gives 40 graded recommendations plus 8 formal uncertainty statements and 17 consensus outcomes. The shared age consensus is inventoried once under PICO 8.1 while its section retains 8.1/8.2.
- TIA: separated formal uncertainty from expert preference in PICO 3.1 (TIA clinic versus stroke-unit admission) and PICO 5.1 (advanced versus standard imaging).
- BAO: formal uncertainties for IVT, advanced imaging, and angioplasty/stenting were previously labelled expert consensus. PICO 5 now contains separate formal statements for pc-ASPECTS 7–10 and 0–6; the former refers to the strength/quality in the other treatment PICOs and receives no invented independent grade. Thus 11 formal source blocks become 12 independently represented formal population statements, plus 7 consensus statements.
- ICH: all 61 statements were already present. The ungraded formal PICO 4.2.2 suggestion to consider EVD with intraventricular thrombolysis is classified as a Statement, retaining very low certainty and explicitly unassigned strength, rather than pretending the source makes no suggestion. PICO 2 has one consensus box with three separately represented paragraphs; PICO 6.3 has two consensus statements. Those accounting choices yield 22 consensus rows.
- Secondary prevention: all 8 formal and 5 consensus statements were already present. Empty formal columns for PICO 2/4/7/10/12 were not filled from narrative uncertainty paragraphs. HbA1c units retain <53 mmol/mol (7%); the source parenthetical glucose-equivalent 154 mg/dL is omitted to avoid presenting glucose units as HbA1c units.
- PFO: all 22 statements were present. PICO 5 prints Weak against while its wording favors DAPT followed by single therapy; this discrepancy is retained without the previous speculative explanation. The PICO 6 24-hour ECG consensus uses age 18–60 in Table 12 but <55 in the body on page 824. The row explicitly records the discrepancy and follows Table 12; no harmonized age threshold was invented. The PDF Table 12 continuation on page 826 was rendered and visually checked.
- Added separately dated current-evidence notes for historical 2021 TNK selection statements (ESO 2023 update) and 2025 andexanet guidance (FDA US risk/availability update). Source grading remains historical and unchanged. Repaired pre-existing replacement-character dashes from primary-source ranges.
