# Guidelines completeness and usability release — 2026-09-06

## Scope and source verification (S1–S3)

This release replaces selected recommendations in the named AHA/ACC, ESO/EAN, ESO and ESC sources with complete formal-source inventories, preserves original grading and clinical qualifiers, and renames the visible section to Guidelines. The prior non-Protocol release is v6.25.0; this follow-up is v6.26.0. Protocols remain excluded.

Primary full articles, corrected supplements, individual image-based recommendation boxes and publication corrections were checked. Numbered recommendations, explicit no-recommendation statements and designated consensus statements were inventoried separately. Narrative practice and economic-value statements retain their own classifications. A missing row in a synopsis was not assumed to be absent from the full guideline.

## Usability and implementation

The section is titled Guidelines. Verified documents show source inventory counts; filtering preserves the complete source count separately from matched rows. Evidence badges stack above text on narrow screens, source references are shorter, and physical PDF links use the reviewed edition rather than a different journal's page numbering.

Lossless build-time packing removes repeated JSON field names from the browser bundle while preserving canonical JSON, all data fields and offline access. Round-trip tests compare every canonical source to its reconstructed form. No network dependency was introduced for guideline reading.

## Validation and limits (S5)

Final unit, content, source inventory, build, browser, offline, Protocols snapshot and deployment results are recorded in the release handoff. The initial browser pass covered all then-complete documents at 320, 390, 768 and 1440 px in light/dark modes and confirmed offline rendering and search. Later source additions are rechecked against the final build.

The collection is not certified universally complete or error-free. The 2026 AHA/ASA rehabilitation full article remains inaccessible through available public sources; its official selected chapter summaries cannot establish complete coverage. Five correction notices remain unresolved (regional destination 2021; cardiac brain health 2024; spasticity 2026; two primary-prevention 2024 notices). These are visible in the source records. Other documents without a reconciled inventory do not receive a complete badge. An exploratory NCS AIS neuroprognostication check also identified differences between main-body and summary-table certainty labels and a supplementary mortality inventory that remains to be reviewed; its existing main-body grades were preserved.

The user set a 19:45 Pacific completion deadline. Further source-audit expansion stopped to finish validation and publish the verified changes within that deadline. No patient data, private institutional protocols or authenticated source material were used.
