This ledger has one row for each change to the evidence content in the working tree on branch `claude/stoic-noether-qt632x`, compared with `c6aea55`. ## Scope and method

**Window.** The search covered evidence published or presented from 2025-01-01 to 2026-10-31.

**Seven domain sweeps.** Each sweep proposed candidate changes, each with its source and a passage from it:
1. IVT and acute systems of care
2. EVT and acute blood pressure
3. Hemorrhagic stroke (ICH, SAH, CVT and vascular malformations)
4. Secondary prevention
5. Cardioembolic stroke and AF
6. Recovery and special populations
7. Trials-screener registry status

An independent adversarial verifier followed each sweep. Each verifier had not written the proposals, re-read the sources and returned accept, modify (with final wording) or reject.
- Of 94 proposals, 90 were accepted (14 accept, 76 modify) and 4 rejected.
- Five accepted items were report-only Protocols notes, so 85 were implemented.
- For a "modify" verdict, the verifier's final wording was used exactly, adapted only to the JSON structure.

**Further pass on missed items.** The verifiers listed 35 items the sweeps had missed. A further verified pass applied five new card-text changes and one record-only source from that list (§8). It also revised three earlier changes, whose final wording appears in their domain rows: ICH BP by hematoma location, ENRICH-AF and the STABLED safety signals. The rest were deferred (§ Deferred).

**Rules applied.**
- Guideline COR and LOE are given as published.
- Evidence that postdates a guideline is labelled as postdating it and carries no grade.
- No conference-only result is used on a card.
- A source record was re-dated to `checkedAt` 2026-10-31 only if it was read that day. Its `access` field states what was read: PubMed abstract, PMC full text or registry record.
- AHA/ASA 2026 AIS recommendation text still comes from the app's archived transcription (`git show 4f8e99d:src/guidelines/ais-2026.json`), which is secondary.

**Access in this session.**
- Worked: the PubMed connector (abstracts, metadata and PMC full text) and the ClinicalTrials.gov connector (full registry records and index queries).
- Unreachable, by DNS failure or a CONNECT 403 from the proxy:
  - ClinicalTrials.gov web pages, the direct API and version history.
  - PubMed web pages and E-utilities.
  - Journals: nejm.org, ahajournals.org, jamanetwork.com and cambridge.org.
  - ESC and NIH: escardio.org, ninds.nih.gov, nih.gov, nihstrokenet.org and captiva.neurosurgery.ufl.edu.
  - Professional and guideline sites: professional.heart.org, guidelinecentral.com and strokebestpractices.ca.
  - News and company sites: medscape.com, hcplive.com, neurologylive.com, medicalxpress.com, brightsurf.com, radcliffecardiology.com, neuronewsinternational.com, auntminnie.com, fiercepharma.com, bayer.com, pharmaphorum.com, pharmashots.com, finance.yahoo.com, trial.medpath.com and ir.mobia.com.
- Web-search result summaries were seen for some of these. They were never treated as verbatim sources, and no shipped text rests on them.
