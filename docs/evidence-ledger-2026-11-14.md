# Evidence ledger: evidence-currency update (accessed 2026-11-14)

This ledger has one row for each change to the Evidence content on branch `impl2-evidence`, compared with `f31a84a` (7.11.1). It covers the topic cards (`src/reference/acute-topics.json`, `clinic-topics.json`, `expanded-care.json`, `expanded-vascular.json`), one study-card limits sentence (`studies.json`) and the source records (`study-sources.json`, `coverage.json`). The previous ledger is [evidence-ledger-2026-10-31.md](evidence-ledger-2026-10-31.md). No Protocols file was changed, and `test:protocol-source` passes.

## Scope and method

**Window.** Evidence published or indexed from 2026-10-25 to 2026-11-14, plus items the 2026-10-31 sweep missed, and accuracy spot-checks of the shipped cards. The audit (findings EV-CUR-01 to EV-CUR-08 and EV-ACC-01) proposed the changes; this pass applied them.

**Re-reading.** Before writing each new sentence, the implementer re-read the PubMed record on 2026-11-14 with the PubMed connector (abstract, and PMC full text where named) and used only numbers visible there. Registry facts come from the ClinicalTrials.gov connector and appear on a card only for LIBREXIA-STROKE. Eligibility details that appear only in a registry record (for example OPTIMAL-STROKE's SBP entry range) were left off the cards.

**Rules applied.**
- Guideline COR and LOE are given as published; no grade was changed.
- Evidence that postdates a guideline is labelled as postdating it and carries no grade ("not graded").
- No conference-only result, press report or news summary is used on a card.
- A source record carries `checkedAt` 2026-11-14 only if it was read that day, and its `access` field says what was read.
- Each new trial name is a keyword of the card that cites it, because global search (Ctrl+K) indexes titles, keywords and summaries, not review items.

**Access in this session.** The PubMed connector (search, metadata and PMC full text) and the ClinicalTrials.gov connector worked. No web page, press release or regulatory site was used. The audit had already recorded that www.fda.gov did not resolve and that accessdata.fda.gov and dailymed.nlm.nih.gov returned CONNECT 403, so the asundexian label could not be read (see "Checked, no change").

### Structural changes made to fit the content limits

`scripts/validate-reference.mjs` caps `consider` at four items.
- `basilar-occlusion` and `maternal-stroke` had three items; each gained a fourth.
- `lipid-management` already had four items. The audit suggested joining two generic items, but the card has only one. Lp(a)HORIZON was instead appended to the triglyceride item (consider[1]), so both non-LDL lipid targets sit together and no existing wording changed.
- SIMPLAAFY was appended to `af-prevention.consider[0]`, STREAM to `primary-prevention.consider[2]` after STAREE, and OPTIMAL-STROKE to `bp-prevention.consider[3]` after ESPRIT; the earlier text of each item is unchanged.
- In `af-prevention.summary` the ASAP-TOO sentence sits before the INVICTUS sentence, not straight after the LAAO-grade sentence. The pinned CLOSURE-AF sentence begins "Postdating these", and putting ASAP-TOO between them would have changed what "these" refers to.
